# Code of conduct

Be direct, patient, and specific. Discuss the work, not the worth of the person
doing it. Harassment, threats, discrimination, sexualized attention, deliberate
misgendering, doxxing, and sustained disruption are not accepted in project
spaces.

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, close threads, reject contributions,
or restrict participation when conduct makes the project unsafe or unusable.
Context, severity, and repeated behavior matter.

Report conduct concerns privately through the maintainer's GitHub profile
contact. Do not post personal or sensitive details in a public issue. Reports
will be reviewed as privately as the available channel permits. Retaliation
against a reporter is itself a violation.
